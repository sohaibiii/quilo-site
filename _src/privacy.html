Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

The short version
  • You don't need an account. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your chat history is stored on your iPhone, not on our servers.
  • To answer you, the message you send (and any photo or PDF you attach) is passed to an AI service: Anthropic or OpenRouter. Quilo asks for your permission before the first time this happens.
  • Answers made with Apple's on-device model never leave your iPhone.
  • We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

This policy explains what information the Quilo app ("Quilo", "we", "us") handles, why, and the choices you have. Quilo is published on the App Store by TOPSOL Innovative Solutions, which is responsible for your information. If you have a question, email us at {{EMAIL}}.

1. Information we handle

Your chats, photos and documents

When you send a message, the text of your message, the earlier messages in that conversation (so the AI has context), and any photo, image or PDF you attach are sent over an encrypted connection to our server and passed on to the AI service that writes the answer (see section 3). The answer is streamed back to your iPhone.

Our server does not save the content of your messages, attachments or answers. It handles them only while your answer is being written.

Your conversations are saved only on your iPhone. You can delete one chat, or all chats from Settings, at any time. Deleting the app also deletes them.

On-device answers

On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, Quilo can answer with Apple's on-device model, for example when you are offline or choose the On-device model. These messages are processed entirely on your iPhone and are not sent to us or to any AI service. If you later ask a cloud model to answer in the same chat, Quilo asks before sending that chat.

App identifier

Quilo creates a random identifier for your installation and stores it in your iPhone's Keychain. It is not linked to your name, Apple Account or advertising identifier. We use it to count your daily free and Pro usage limits, to prevent abuse, and to check whether you have an active Quilo Pro subscription. On our server we keep daily usage counts (for example, how many messages, photos and images you used that day) against this identifier, together with short fingerprints of attached files so that the same file is not counted twice. We do not keep the files themselves.

When we pass a request to an AI service, we send a one-way scrambled version of this identifier rather than the identifier itself, so the service can detect abuse without knowing who you are.

Network and location information

Like any internet service, our server receives your IP address. We use it to limit the number of requests to protect the service from abuse, and we do not store it with your chats. When you turn on web search, we use your approximate location (city, region, country and time zone, worked out from your IP address by our hosting provider) so that search results are relevant to where you are. We do not use precise GPS location.

Purchases

Subscriptions are bought and paid for through Apple. We never see your payment card or Apple Account details. We receive confirmation from Apple, through our subscription partner RevenueCat, of whether your installation has an active subscription, which plan, and when it renews or ends.

Reports

If you use Report on an answer or a created image, we receive the reason you chose, the model that was used, and a shortened copy of your request and of the answer (up to about 1,000 and 4,000 characters), along with your app identifier. We use reports to find and fix harmful or wrong answers. Reports are kept in our server logs for a limited time (see section 5).

Camera, photo library and speech

Quilo uses the camera and photo library only when you choose to take or pick a photo, and only the photos you choose are used. Quilo does not use your microphone. Read aloud uses your iPhone's built-in voices on the device.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, or advertising identifier. Quilo contains no ads, no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and no cross-app tracking.

2. How we use information

  • To answer your questions, analyze your photos and documents, search the web and create images you ask for.
  • To apply the free plan's daily limits and to provide Quilo Pro to subscribers.
  • To keep the service secure and protect it from fraud and abuse.
  • To review reported content and improve the safety and quality of answers.
  • To reply when you contact support.

We do not use your chats to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or rent personal information.

3. AI services and other providers

Quilo shares information only with the service providers that run the app for us. Before your first message, image or regenerated answer, Quilo shows a screen that names these AI services and asks for your permission. You can turn sharing off at any time in Settings, Data, "Share chats with AI services". With sharing off, Quilo can only answer with the on-device model where available.

4. Please don't share sensitive information

AI answers can be wrong. Avoid sending passwords, payment details, health records or other sensitive personal information to Quilo, and don't rely on Quilo for medical, legal, financial or other professional advice.

5. How long we keep information

  • Chats and attachments: on your iPhone until you delete them. Not stored on our server.
  • Daily usage counts and file fingerprints: kept while the app identifier is in use, and only as long as needed to apply limits.
  • Server logs, including reports: kept for a short period, normally no more than 30 days, then deleted.
  • Support emails: kept as long as needed to help you, then deleted.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete any chat, or all chats, in the app.
  • Turn off sharing with AI services in Settings, Data.
  • Deny or remove camera and photo access in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because Quilo has no accounts, most of your data lives only on your iPhone, where you can delete it yourself. For anything we hold, we may need details that help us find your records, since we don't know who you are. Email {{EMAIL}} and we will respond within 30 days. We will not treat you differently for using your rights.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on performing our contract with you (answering your questions and providing Pro), your consent (sharing chats with AI services, which you can withdraw in Settings), and our legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention and reviewing reports).

7. International transfers

Our providers process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Security

All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted with HTTPS. AI service keys are kept on our server, never in the app. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. Children

Quilo is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Quilo changes, for example if we add a new AI service. We will change the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
