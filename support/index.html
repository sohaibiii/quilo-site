Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Quilo Pro, then Cancel Subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or current period ends to avoid the next charge. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

All payments go through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, choose the Quilo purchase and select "Request a refund".

I paid but Quilo still shows the free plan

Open Quilo, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe and that you're online. If it still doesn't work, email us.

How does the free trial work?

The weekly plan starts with a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. If you don't cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, it turns into a paid weekly subscription at the price shown in the app.

What is Auto mode?

Auto mode picks the best AI model for each message: a fast model for everyday chats, and a stronger one for photos, documents, writing, code and math. You can also pick a model yourself from the model menu at the top of the chat.

Why did I hit a daily limit?

The free plan includes a set number of messages, photos and documents each day, and resets daily. Quilo Pro raises these limits. Pro also has fair-use limits on the most advanced model and on image creation.

Can I use Quilo offline?

On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, Quilo can answer with Apple's on-device model when you're offline. Photos, PDFs, web search, image creation and the cloud models need an internet connection.

Where are my chats stored? How do I delete them?

Your chat history is stored only on your iPhone. Press and hold a chat in the history list to delete it, or go to Settings and tap Delete all chats. Read the Privacy Policy for what is sent to AI services to answer you.

How do I stop sharing my chats with AI services?

Go to Settings, Data, and turn off Share chats with AI services. Quilo will then only answer with the on-device model where it's available.

I got a wrong or harmful answer

Use Report (the flag) on the answer or image. Reports help us make Quilo safer. AI answers can be wrong, so double-check anything important.

Does Quilo work on iPad or Mac?

Quilo is made for iPhone today. Versions for more devices may come later.
